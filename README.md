# Stranger Things 🎬

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction horror series created by *The Duffer Brothers*.  
It premiered on **Netflix** in 2016 and quickly became a global phenomenon.

---

## 🌟 Why I Love It
- Nostalgic 1980s setting
- Strong friendships and character development
- Thrilling mix of horror, sci-fi, and mystery

---

### Main Characters
1. **Eleven** – A girl with telekinetic powers  
2. **Mike Wheeler** – Loyal friend and leader  
3. **Jim Hopper** – The town’s tough but caring sheriff  

---

### Favorite Quote
> "Friends don’t lie." – Eleven
